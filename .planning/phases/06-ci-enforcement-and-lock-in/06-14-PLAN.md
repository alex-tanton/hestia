## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): "CI blocks regressions of everything this milestone cleaned, without turning `main` red or breaking the `CI`-triggers-deploy contract"

Gap being closed (06-UAT.md, G-06-5, blocker): "CI is reliable: no test in CI fails intermittently". This plan is part (b) of the owner's instruction: root-cause each flaky test by reading the spec and the app code and by reproducing it, with no blind retries. It ends at the owner's gate because a root cause may be a product race (a real user could hit it), and a product change needs the owner's approval and a regression test (PROJECT.md: behavior changes only for documented bugs). OD-5 supersedes DA-4, so the notification specs are in scope.

What the planning-time scan of CI history saw (06-11 replaces this with the full inventory): `19-notifications.spec.ts:140` (older lines `:141`, `:166`) failed on about seven attempts, after `page.goto('/meals?household={Oak Street}')` the household switcher still read "Rivera Family"; `17-accessibility-states.spec.ts:497` (older `:498`) about five times, "Couldn't save that setting. Try again." never appeared; `17-accessibility-states.spec.ts:526` (older `:527`) about three times, `Expected: >= 0, Received: -1` (no switch was off), twice in the same attempt as a 17:497 failure; `17-accessibility-states.spec.ts:320` and `18-chores-and-errands-offline.spec.ts:140` once each. All with `retries: 0` and no registry error. These are leads for this plan to confirm or refute, not conclusions.

MVP mode asks for a user story. The goal is outcome-shaped but not written as a user story, so it is not reframed here (no story is invented).

<objective>
Find the cause of every flaky test in the inventory from its CI trace and a local reproduction, write each cause down with its evidence, its fix and the regression test that will prove the fix, and get the owner's approval of the fixes, the proof bar and the re-run budget in one reply.

Purpose: a fix without a known cause is a guess, and the owner ruled out retries, skips and loosened tests. A measured local failure rate before the fix is also the baseline the fix is proven against in 06-15.

Output: `## Root causes` in FLAKE-INVENTORY.md, `rc-F-nn=` lines, `diagnosis=done`, and the owner's gate reply with the final `proof-bar-ci=` and `proof-reruns=`.
</objective>
