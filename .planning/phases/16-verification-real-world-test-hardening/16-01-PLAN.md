  <action>
    Expand `apps/service/src/deals/price/parse-price.test.ts` to exhaustively test complex pricing math, edge cases, and guardrails per D-01:
    1. Multi-buy promotions:
       - "4 for $5.00" -> unitPrice 125, displayPrice "4 for $5.00"
       - "2/$6.00 MUST BUY 2" -> unitPrice 300, priceUnit "each"
       - "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" at currentPrice 199 -> unitPrice 133, originalPrice 199, discountPercent 33
       - "Buy 1 Get 1 50% Off" -> unitPrice 75% of original, discountPercent 25
    2. Minimum quantity thresholds:
       - "with min purchase of 3" -> qualifies displayPrice, calculates unit price correctly
    3. Per-weight unit price rounding:
       - "$1.99/lb" and fractional calculations round cents cleanly without floating point precision artifacts
    4. Boundary values and guardrails:
       - Sale price equal to regular price -> drops originalPrice to null, discountPercent null
       - Sale price greater than regular price (negative discount) -> drops originalPrice to null
       - 10x case price drop (originalPrice >= 10x unitPrice, e.g. OCR misread $23.90 for $2.39/lb) -> drops originalPrice to null
       - Excessive discount (>95%, e.g. unitPrice $0.04 vs original $1.00) -> drops originalPrice to null
    5. Direct Price and Qualifiers:
       - "FINAL PRICE 2/$4.00" with digital coupon flag and mixed copy strings
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>pnpm --filter @hestia/service test src/deals/price/parse-price.test.ts</automated>
    <fails_when>Unit tests in parse-price.test.ts fail.</fails_when>
  </verify>
  <done>
    All new pricing test cases pass in Vitest, confirming complete coverage of multi-buy math, Buy-N-Get-M derivations, and boundary guardrails.
  </done>
</task>
