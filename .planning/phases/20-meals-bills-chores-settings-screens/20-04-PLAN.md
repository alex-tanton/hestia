<objective>
Overhaul the Settings, Diagnostics & Departure workflows onto Material Design 3 surfaces. Replace SettingsScreen's monolithic stacked form wall with a categorized md-list hub navigating to pushed sub-routes (/settings/account, /settings/households, /settings/notifications, /settings/system-health), each maintaining single-<h1> Top App Bar back navigation. Modernize notification preferences with a top tonal device guidance banner and grouped Switch controls. Convert FavoriteStores into removable MdFilterChip pills with an md-list store picker dialog. Redesign SystemHealthScreen as a borderless diagnostic panel, and streamline DepartureScreen into a progressive single-column flow with MdSelect reassignments and error-styled ConfirmDialog verification.

Purpose: Provide an uncluttered settings navigation hierarchy, eliminate monolithic configuration walls, and ensure safe, progressive household reassignments.
Output: apps/web/src/screens/settings/SettingsScreen.tsx, AccountRoute.tsx, HouseholdsRoute.tsx, NotificationsRoute.tsx, NotificationTypes.tsx, FavoriteStores.tsx, SystemHealthScreen.tsx, and DepartureScreen.tsx.
</objective>
