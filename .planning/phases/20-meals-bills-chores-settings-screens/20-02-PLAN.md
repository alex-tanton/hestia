Overhaul the Bills & Subscriptions pillar onto Material Design 3 surfaces. Modernize BillsIndexScreen with urgency-grouped sections (Overdue, Upcoming, Inactive), floating MdFab for bill creation, and direct one-tap "Mark Paid" row actions backed by persistent single-slot Undo Snackbars (eliminating legacy 1-item overflow menus).
