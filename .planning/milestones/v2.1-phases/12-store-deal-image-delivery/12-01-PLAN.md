<objective>
Centralize image CDN domain definitions and strict URL validation in `@hestia/shared`, and configure Caddy reverse proxy ingress Content-Security-Policy `img-src` to permit modern Flipp and Open Food Facts CDNs.
</objective>

<context>
Modern Flipp flyers and circular items distribute assets across multiple subdomains (`*.flipp.com`, `flipp-image-retrieval.flipp.com`, `*.wishabi.com`, `*.wishabi.net`), and Open Food Facts product items resolve from `*.openfoodfacts.org`. Previously, Hestia's ingress CSP and component allowlists were hardcoded to only two legacy hosts (`https://f.wishabi.net` and `https://images.wishabi.net`), causing images to be blocked at the browser network boundary or rejected by UI components.

Per decisions D-01, D-02, and D-04:
- D-01: Centralize allowed image domain definitions and URL validation in `@hestia/shared` as a single source of truth.
- D-02: Enforce strict `protocol === 'https:'` and dot-subdomain suffix validation (`hostname === domain || hostname.endsWith('.' + domain)`) against `['flipp.com', 'wishabi.com', 'wishabi.net', 'openfoodfacts.org']`.
- D-04: Update `docker/Caddyfile` `img-src` directive to include CSP Level 3 wildcards for all allowed origins.
</context>

<threat_model>
ASVS Level: 1
Block on: high

- **T-12-01: Domain injection, spoofing, and insecure mixed-content bypass.**
  - *Threat:* An attacker provides crafted image URLs such as `https://attacker-flipp.com/image.png`, `https://flipp.com.attacker.com/image.png`, or insecure `http://flipp.com/flyer.jpg` to bypass origin validation, trigger mixed-content warnings, or execute cross-origin tracking.
  - *Mitigation:* Strict WHATWG `URL` parsing, enforcing `protocol === 'https:'`, and anchoring hostname checks to exact equality or a dot-prefixed suffix (`hostname.endsWith('.' + domain)`) against `ALLOWED_IMAGE_DOMAINS`.
- **T-12-02: Ingress CSP bypass and malicious external resource loading.**
  - *Threat:* An overly permissive CSP directive (such as `img-src *` or `https:`) could allow data exfiltration or arbitrary external asset rendering in the PWA.
  - *Mitigation:* `docker/Caddyfile` sets an explicit CSP Level 3 origin allowlist (`img-src 'self' data: https://*.flipp.com https://flipp-image-retrieval.flipp.com https://*.wishabi.com https://*.wishabi.net https://*.openfoodfacts.org;`), restricting network image retrieval to approved CDN origins while rejecting insecure HTTP and unlisted third-party hosts.
</threat_model>

<specless_assumptions>
- **IMG-01:** Caddy CSP wildcard origins `*.flipp.com`, `*.wishabi.com`, `*.wishabi.net`, `*.openfoodfacts.org` and apex retrieval host `flipp-image-retrieval.flipp.com` exhaustively cover all active Flipp flyer endpoints and Open Food Facts product image servers.
- **IMG-03:** `isAllowedImageUrl` accepts `string`, `URL`, `null`, or `undefined` inputs, returning `false` cleanly without throwing on malformed strings, relative paths, or non-URL values.
</specless_assumptions>
